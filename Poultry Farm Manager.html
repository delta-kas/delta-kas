🐣 Day-1 chick quality audit –

Check 20–30 random chicks before/until 24 h post-placement. Tick each point passed.

Chick crop fill check (first 24 hours)

Time after placementTarget full cropsAction if low
2 hours75% chicks fullVerify chick paper / feed tray coverage
8 hours85% chicks fullAdjust drinker line height
24 hours95–100% fullCheck brooding temp & water flow
  • Soft dough crop = optimal (feed + water found) ✅
  • Hard crop = found feed, no water → check drinkers
  • Watery crop = found water, no feed → check feed access
  • Empty crop = starvation risk → act immediately
